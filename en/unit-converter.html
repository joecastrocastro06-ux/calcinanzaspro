How it works

What this calculator does

This converter handles the six kinds of units people convert most often: length, weight, temperature, area, volume and speed. It uses exact, internationally defined factors, shows the formula it applied and converts your value into every unit of the category at once.

The formula

Linear units: result = value × (factorfrom ÷ factorto), with each factor expressed in the base unit (m, kg, m², L, m/s)
°C → °F: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 · °F → °C: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
K = °C + 273.15

Worked example, step by step

  1. 10 miles to kilometers: 1 mi = 1,609.344 m = 1.609344 km, so 10 × 1.609344 = 16.09344 km.
  2. 5 lb to kg: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, so 5 × 0.45359237 = 2.2680 kg.
  3. 68 °F to °C: (68 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 20 °C; in kelvin, 20 + 273.15 = 293.15 K.

Frequently asked questions

How precise are the conversions?

The factors are exact by definition (for example 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg since 1959). Results are shown with up to 10 significant digits.

Why are US and UK gallons different?

The US gallon is 3.785 liters; the imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 liters. US cups, tablespoons and fluid ounces also differ slightly from their UK counterparts.

Why can’t temperature be converted with a single factor?

Because Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points. You need to shift and scale: multiply by 9/5 and add 32 to go from °C to °F.

How many square feet are in an acre?

Exactly 43,560 square feet, or about 4,046.86 m². A hectare is 10,000 m², roughly 2.47 acres.
