Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

This website, calcfinanzaspro.com (“CalcFinanzasPro”, “we”, “us”), offers free online calculators in English, Spanish, Portuguese and German. CalcFinanzasPro is the data controller for the processing described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, write to contacto@calcfinanzaspro.com.

2. The numbers you enter in our calculators stay on your device

All calculators on this site run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The amounts, rates, salaries, debts, weights or any other values you type are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers or to any third party. We do not store, log, share or sell this information, and it disappears when you close or reload the page. The “Copy result” button only places the text on your own clipboard.

3. Information collected automatically

Like any website, our hosting provider, Netlify, Inc., automatically processes technical data needed to deliver the pages and keep the service secure, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time of the request. This data is used only for security and to operate the site. See Netlify’s privacy policy.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, address or payment details.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Our calculators themselves do not need cookies to work. However, this site shows advertising through Google AdSense, and therefore:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to calcfinanzaspro.com or to other websites.
  • These cookies and similar technologies (such as device identifiers and local storage) are also used to limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

5. Google advertising cookies

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Google may also allow certified third-party ad vendors and ad networks to serve ads on this site; they may use their own cookies for the same purposes. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. How to opt out of personalized advertising

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings: myadcenter.google.com/ or www.google.com/settings/ads.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. Users in Europe can also use www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

If you visit us from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through a consent message before cookies are used for personalized advertising, as required by the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information. To opt out of sharing for personalized ads, use the options in section 6 or contact us.

To exercise any of these rights, email contacto@calcfinanzaspro.com. We will reply within the time limits set by law.
